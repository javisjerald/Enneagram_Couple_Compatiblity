Couple Assessment Control Panel

Important: ECCRA is an original Enneagram-informed relationship screening and counselling-formulation aid. It is not a validated psychometric instrument, not an official Gottman/Prepare-Enrich/Enneagram Institute assessment, and it does not calculate whether two Enneagram types are “meant” for each other.

Each partner completes the assessment independently. Type information is used as a discussion lens, not as a compatibility verdict. Safety is interpreted separately and can never be explained away by personality type.
